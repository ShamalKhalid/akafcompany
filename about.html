About AKAF Company

AKAF Company is a distinguished multi-disciplinary engineering, manpower, and industrial services provider headquartered in Al Jubail, the industrial heart of the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. For more than 14 years, we have actively supported the nation's growth by delivering end-to-end technical solutions to the Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Construction, Infrastructure, Power & Energy, Logistics, and Government sectors.

Since our inception, AKAF has been built on a commitment to workmanship, innovation, reliability, and safety. Our capabilities continue to expand, allowing us to serve complex industrial requirements — from long-term project staffing and large-scale shutdown maintenance to specialised civil & mechanical works, EPC assistance, and heavy equipment rental.

Driven by a dedication to safety, quality, and operational excellence, we partner with clients to ensure seamless project execution, enhanced productivity, and sustained long-term performance.

A Partner in National Industrial Transformation

Saudi Arabia is undergoing rapid and historic transformation, and AKAF Company is proud to be part of major industrial development initiatives supporting Vision 2030. Our multicultural workforce of engineers, consultants, technicians, operators, and skilled workers has earned a reputation for professionalism, timely execution, and superior service delivery. Today, we serve both local and international EPC contractors and end users across the Gulf — from Al Jubail and Yanbu to Riyadh and the Eastern Province.

Our Workforce Is Our Strength

We believe people are the foundation of every successful project. AKAF supports clients with a practical workforce model built around:

  • Certified engineers and project managers
  • Skilled technicians across mechanical, electrical, and instrumentation disciplines
  • Welders, fabricators, scaffolders, and field support crews
  • QA/QC inspectors and HSE professionals
  • Rapid mobilisation aligned with project and shutdown requirements

Our workforce approach is designed to match the right people to the right industrial scope, safely and efficiently.

Built on Quality, Safety & Technical Excellence

AKAF combines quality workforce delivery with disciplined project execution. Our operating approach emphasises:

  • HSE compliance and a Zero-Accident culture
  • Real-time coordination with clients and contractors
  • Industrial and commercial project execution across civil, mechanical, and electrical works
  • Turnkey, subcontracting, and project support capability

This practical, site-focused approach helps clients maintain quality, continuity, and performance across active operations.
