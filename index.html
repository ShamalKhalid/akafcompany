About Us

AKAF Company is a Saudi-based industrial support services provider delivering reliable manpower, engineering support, and project services to Oil & Gas, Petrochemical, Construction, Infrastructure, Power & Energy, Logistics, and Government projects across Saudi Arabia.

With more than 14 years of experience, we support clients with certified engineers and project managers, skilled technicians, welders, fabricators, scaffolders, QA/QC inspectors, and HSE professionals.

We focus on safe execution, dependable manpower mobilisation, quality project delivery, and practical industrial support that helps clients improve performance and build long-term value.

  • Certified engineers and project managers
  • Skilled technicians across mechanical, electrical, and instrumentation disciplines
  • Welders, fabricators, scaffolders, and field support crews
  • QA/QC inspectors, HSE professionals, and rapid workforce mobilisation
